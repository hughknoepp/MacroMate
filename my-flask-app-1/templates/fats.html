These are estimates, not medical advice. Check with a doctor or dietitian before making big changes to how you eat, especially if you have high cholesterol, heart disease or another medical condition.

Making it work

Choose mostly unsaturated fats: olive and canola oil, nuts, seeds and avocado. Eat fatty fish, like salmon or sardines, about twice a week.

Keep saturated fat (butter, fatty meat, cheese, coconut and palm oil) low, and avoid trans fats as much as you can.

Fat has 9 calories per gram, more than twice the 4 in protein or carbs, so small portions add up quickly.

How these numbers are worked out

{% if not rec.minor %}

For each goal, the middle of its calorie suggestion is used.

  • Total fat: 20–35% of calories, the usual healthy range for adults. Going below 20% has no benefit and can make it harder to get essential fats and vitamins.
  • Saturated fat: under 10% of calories.
  • Fits with the others: your carb targets are what remains after your protein and this much fat.

Amounts are rounded to the nearest 5 g, except the saturated fat limit, which is rounded down to stay under 10%.
